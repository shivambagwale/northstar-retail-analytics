# Interview Notes

## Main analytical findings

- Top 20% of customers contributed approximately 33.9% of revenue.
- Repeat customer rate was 99.8%.
- Weekend basket values were significantly higher than weekday basket values.
- Product and store revenue distribution showed broad performance across categories and locations.

## Data quality observations

- Mixed date formats required cleaning.
- Store naming conventions were inconsistent.
- customers.csv contained 535 rows while imported MySQL customer table contained 506 rows.
- Referential integrity checks showed no missing transaction references.
